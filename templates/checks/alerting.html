How delivery works

An alert rule fires when its condition is met, which records an alert event and opens an incident. The event is then delivered to every enabled channel attached to that rule. A channel that fails is logged and skipped, so one broken destination cannot block the others.

Outbound URLs (Slack, webhook and the SMS gateway) must match ALERT_WEBHOOK_ALLOWLIST, and are refused otherwise.
